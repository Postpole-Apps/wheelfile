## What stays on your device

Your vehicle logs are stored on your device. Fill-ups, charges, services, expenses, reminders, documents, receipts, photos, plates, VINs and notes are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Postpole has no account system and no server that receives them. You can export a backup or CSV at any time, and delete documents and reminders from the app.

## What is collected by the SDKs in the app

- **Ads (Google AdMob).** The free version shows ads. Google's ads SDK can collect and share your Advertising ID, app-set ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interactions and diagnostics, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first (Google User Messaging Platform) and you can change your choice later in Settings, Privacy options. Buying Remove ads or Pro turns ads off.
- **Analytics (Firebase Analytics).** Counts of actions such as saving an entry or opening the Pro screen. Event parameters are fixed labels and counts. They never contain plates, VINs, notes or amounts.
- **Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).** Technical crash data to fix bugs. It does not include your logs.
- **Remote Config (Firebase).** Settings that control how often ads can show.
- **Purchases (Google Play Billing).** Google Play handles payment. The app learns which product you own so it can unlock it.

## Your choices

- Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.
- Change your ad consent in Settings, Privacy options.
- Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to remove all of its data from your device.
- US state residents may have rights to know, delete and opt out of sharing. Write to the contact address above.

## Children

Wheelfile is for adults and is not directed to children under 13.
